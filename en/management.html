Your UK property looked after
wherever you live

JoyHome Property Management (JHPM) is our sister company, founded in January 2021 and a member of UKALA and the PRS. We let, collect rent and manage homes for landlords investing in the UK.

Service two

Full tenancy management

From safety certificates before the tenancy to repairs and legal notices during it, we handle it all.

  • Gas and electrical safety certificates before the tenancy, with the gas certificate renewed yearly
  • A photo and written inventory before move-in, used when the deposit is returned
  • Monthly rent collection
  • Handling tenant queries and repairs and keeping you informed
  • Reliable contractors for repairs, with your approval
  • A signed photo record of every key issued
  • Holding keys and all property documents during the tenancy
  • Regular inspections, usually every 6 months
  • Serving legal notices for rent increases, renewals or ending a tenancy

FAQ

About property management

What landlords ask before appointing us.

Can't find your question? Ask us
How do I appoint JHPM?

We send you a management agreement covering the address, description, expected rent, our fees and terms. You add your details and the bank account for rent, sign it and send it back, and we can start.

Does JHPM inspect the property?

Yes. We usually inspect every 6 months and send you a report.

I live overseas. Can you manage my property?

Yes. JHPM has helped many overseas landlords and is registered with the Non-Resident Landlord Scheme, which makes it easier for you to register as a non-resident landlord.

Have a property to let?

Tell us the address and type and we'll estimate the rent and our fees.
